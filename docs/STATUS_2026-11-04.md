## 3. Critique: why the features do not feel like one product

1. **Islands with no bridges.** The scheme finder never leads to "check my claim", although five of its
   seven schemes are insurance (PMSBY, PMJJBY, PMFBY, PM-JAY, Gopinath Munde) and lists "how a claim can
   fail". A claim checked as crop/accident/life gets a general plan and no link to the scheme page that
   describes that cover.
2. **Real cases are a dead end.** RealCases on the result links out to Indian Kanoon only. `/cases` does
   not know your claim (no filter by your reason) and never offers "check a claim like this".
3. **Evidence is invisible.** The product's claim is "measured against real judgments", but nothing in
   the app or README shows a measured number. README still says "pending" everywhere.
4. **Same content in three places.** The result screen, `/action-plan` and `/results/:id` each re-render
   the plan, deadlines and real cases. This is not fixed here (that needs a UI decision, which waits for
   the motion template); the pages now link to each other.
5. **The flow got longer, not shorter.** You asked Qoder for less "answer this, answer that"; the claim
   flow went from 3 steps to 5. Left for the UI pass.
6. **Two front doors.** The landing page has scheme CTAs four times and claim CTAs twice, although you
   told Qoder the claim check is the baseline. Left for the UI pass.

## 5. What this pass did (4 Nov)

**Data bug fixed (serious).** `gallery/*.json` carried only the raw disposal word: 8 of 9 cases said
"dismissed", and RealCases painted "dismissed" as a loss. The direction-corrected winner
(`case.policyholder_prevailed`, the same field the eval uses) shows the policyholder won 7 of 9. The gallery
now carries `policyholder_prevailed`, everything groups by who won, and the gallery was rebuilt.

**Answer first, evidence cited (the OpenEvidence pattern).** The result screen now reads:
short answer (verdict counts + one line per finding with a `[n]` marker) > plain-words explanation >
numbered evidence cards (rule text with a "checked word for word against the stored source" line, and the
document lines used) > what to do next (plan, deadlines, the complaint-date form and the letter together)
> similar real cases.

**Charts replaced.** DecisionFlow (boxes that repeated rule ids), the Plotly bar + 3-D terrain (at most 4
points per reason, categorical z axis) and the Plotly deadline Gantt are gone, and Plotly (~3 MB) was
uninstalled. In their place: a headline "the policyholder won X of Y" + a segmented bar + the cited list;
a deadline timeline with every date written on it and "N days left" / "opens in N days". The retired files
are in the session scratchpad, not deleted from history (they were never committed).

**Bridges.** Scheme finder > claim check with the type preset (`/check-claim?type=`; PMSBY accident, PMJJBY
life, PMFBY crop; PM-JAY and Gopinath Munde left out pending Parth). Non-health result > scheme finder.
Result > `/cases?ground=<your reason>` (filtered list first, who won on every case, "check a claim like
this"). New `/evidence` page.

**Evidence.** `cli eval` now also writes `docs/eval/summary.json`; `GET /api/evidence` serves it (not
`/evidence`, which is the page: Qoder's plan had both on one path). README rules column filled from the
report; the Space image copies `docs/eval`.

**Cases page.** No internal ids as titles; DD/MM/YYYY dates; "Our rules check:" label before the stamp.

Tests: backend 1,477 passed; frontend 272 passed; typecheck and build clean. Browser-checked: `/cases`,

## 5b. Impeccable critique and fixes (4 Nov, afternoon)

Critique (design review + detector, two isolated passes): 19/40 on Nielsen heuristics. Root causes found:
one card recipe everywhere (paper #FFFCF7 on ivory #FBF6EE, so boxes exist only as a hairline), nested
boxes, a drifting sunset behind an Operate surface, verdict colours reused for unrelated meanings
(sage = "consistent" and also "policyholder won"), a 4px coloured side stripe (banned), eyebrows above
every heading (banned), the 5-pill rail overflowing on phones, and states styled only on some steps.

Fixed: the answer sentence is the result's heading (large, on one peach band, the only raised object);
counts in one sentence instead of traffic-light tiles; real-case wins kept out of the answer (no false
hope next to "need info"); evidence list flat with hairlines, rule text on paper with a firm edge vs your
document on peach; plan steps cite [n] instead of re-quoting; who-won and deadline bars in neutral ink;
compact result header with the helper toggle; no sunset on /check-claim; no eyebrows; a one-line step
status on phones; pending tags amber on every step; "Change" not "Edit value"; settled/total count bug;
optional letter details folded; delete last, "Check another claim" added; footer leads with the claim
check; /evidence says "None" not "$0.00"; local date for "today".

**Flow merged to three steps (Parth chose this, 4 Nov):** upload > confirm > answer. The confirm page holds
what needs an answer (open, first), the facts read cleanly (folded behind one line), the type of insurance,
missing dates, continuity and reply date, and the optional letter details and policy terms (folded).
"Run the check" validates in order: unanswered facts, then the type of insurance, then the server's 422/409
answers, shown at their fields. ReviewFacts, AdditionalInfo, ReviewSubmit and FactsBoard are retired (moved
to the session scratchpad; never committed). Date inputs use a text keypad so "/" can be typed.
